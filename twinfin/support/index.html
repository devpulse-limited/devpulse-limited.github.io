How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to fly up, and let go to fall. The macaw above the waterline and the clownfish below it mirror each other, so one thumb steers both. Touch anything and the run ends. Tap to try again straight away.

When does the daily level change?

Every day at midnight UTC, which is midnight in London in winter and 1am in summer. Everyone plays the same level that day, so scores are comparable. You can retry it as often as you like.

How do the medals work?

Pass 10 gates in a run for Bronze, 25 for Silver, 50 for Gold and 100 for Platinum. Your best for the day and your all time best are kept on your phone.

Leaderboards

TwinFin uses Apple Game Center for its daily and all time leaderboards. If your scores do not appear, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap Game Center and check that you are signed in. You can play without it.

Sound, music and vibration

Each has its own switch. Tap the gear icon on the home screen.

Something is not right

  • Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store.
  • Close TwinFin from the app switcher and open it again.
  • Still stuck? Email us and we will help.

Privacy

Your scores and settings stay on your phone, and there is no account to create. The privacy policy has the details.
