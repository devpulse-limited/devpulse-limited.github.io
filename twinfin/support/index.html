Support

Help with TwinFin on iPhone

Need a hand?

Email support@devpulse.co.uk. Tell us your iPhone model, the iOS version and what happened, and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days.

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to fly up, let go to fall. The macaw above the waterline and the clownfish below it mirror each other, so one thumb steers both. Touch anything and the run ends; tap to try again.

When does the daily level change?

Every day at 00:00 UTC (midnight in London in winter, 1am in summer). Everyone plays the same level that day, so scores are comparable.

Remove Ads and restoring it

  • Buy: on the home screen tap the gear icon, then Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase; the banner goes straight away.
  • New phone or reinstalled? Tap the gear icon, then Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Leaderboards

TwinFin uses Apple Game Center for its daily and all-time leaderboards. If your scores don't appear, check that you're signed in under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone. You can play without it.

Ad privacy choices

In the UK and Europe you can change your ad consent at any time: tap the gear icon, then Ad privacy choices. Tracking can be turned on or off in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More in the privacy policy.

Sound, music and vibration

Each has its own switch under the gear icon on the home screen.
