Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · applies to TwinFin 1.0 on iPhone

The short version

TwinFin is free with one small banner ad. The ad comes from Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads. You choose whether ads can be personalised, and whether apps may track you, and the game plays exactly the same either way.

If you buy Remove Ads, the ad is gone and Google's ad software never starts. The purchase is handled by Apple and recorded by RevenueCat under an anonymous ID.

We (DevPulse) don't run servers for TwinFin and don't receive personal data about you. Your scores and settings stay on your phone. No account, no analytics.

Who we are

TwinFin is made by DevPulse (Devpulse Limited, devpulse.co.uk), based in the United Kingdom. In this policy "we" and "us" mean DevPulse. For the data the banner ad and the purchase involve, Google and RevenueCat process it as described below and in their own policies.

What stays on your phone

The game keeps a small save file on your device only:

  • your best score for each daily level, your all-time best, how many tries you made each day and the medals you earned;
  • your settings (sound, music and vibration on or off);
  • whether you bought Remove Ads, so the ad stays off when you're offline.

This file is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud) may include it, under Apple's terms.

The daily level is worked out on your phone from today's date (UTC). The game needs no internet connection for that.

The banner ad (Google AdMob)

Unless you've bought Remove Ads, TwinFin shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen, provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app collects:

  • device identifiers: Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (see below), plus identifiers scoped to the app or to us;
  • approximate location, estimated from your IP address (never GPS);
  • advertising and usage data: which ads were shown, taps on them, app launches;
  • diagnostics: crash and performance data of the ad software.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. How Google uses it: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

TwinFin asks Google for ads suitable for a general audience only.

Your choices: consent and tracking

  • Consent (UK, EEA and Switzerland). Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) asks whether Google and its partners may use your data for personalised ads and the other purposes listed there. You can change your answer at any time in TwinFin under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • App Tracking Transparency (all countries). iOS then asks whether TwinFin may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and TwinFin asks Google for non-personalised ads only. You can change this later in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Neither question ever interrupts a run, and saying no doesn't change the game.
  • You can also reset your advertising identifier or turn off personalised ads from Apple under Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Remove Ads (in-app purchase, RevenueCat)

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase. Apple takes the payment; we never see your card or Apple ID details. To check the purchase and restore it on your other devices, TwinFin uses RevenueCat, which receives the purchase receipt and keeps a purchase record (the product, its date and the store's transaction details) under an anonymous ID it creates for the app. We don't give RevenueCat your name, email or any account. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

After you buy Remove Ads, the consent form and the tracking prompt are no longer shown and Google's ad software is not started.

Game Center leaderboards

If you're signed in to Apple Game Center, TwinFin can post your daily score to its leaderboards and show you how you rank. This is provided by Apple, not by us: Apple receives your score and your player profile (such as your nickname) under its own privacy policy, and the leaderboards show your nickname and score to other players. We don't receive your Game Center account details. Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy. You can turn Game Center off, or change who can see you, in the Settings app under Game Center. You can play TwinFin fully without it.

Sharing your score

The Share button opens the iPhone's own share sheet with a picture of your score card. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store on our servers or sell personal data, and there is no analytics or crash-reporting software of our own in the game.
  • TwinFin doesn't ask for your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location. (The iPhone may list some of these because the game engine includes them; the game never uses them.)

App Store

Apple handles downloads and updates and may collect data about that under its own policies. We see the anonymous, aggregated statistics Apple shows every developer (for example downloads per country), which don't identify you.

Legal bases (UK GDPR / GDPR)

  • Personalised ads and the advertising identifier: your consent, through the consent form and the tracking prompt; you can withdraw it at any time as described above.
  • Non-personalised ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free game, and Google's, as described in Google's policies.
  • The Remove Ads purchase record: to perform the contract of the purchase (so it works on your devices and can be restored).

Children

TwinFin is rated for all ages. It doesn't knowingly collect personal data from children, asks Google for ads suitable for a general audience only, and the purchase goes through Apple's own controls (for example Ask to Buy for family accounts).

How long data is kept

The save file stays on your phone until you delete the app. Google and RevenueCat keep the data described above for the periods set out in their policies.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, object to its use and withdraw consent. We hold no personal data about you ourselves; for ad data, use Google's tools (myadcenter.google.com) or contact Google, and for the purchase record write to us and we'll ask RevenueCat to delete it (Apple keeps its own record of your purchases). You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of TwinFin changes what is collected, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will change with it.
