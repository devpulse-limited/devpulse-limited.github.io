Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

The short version

TwinFin is free with one small banner ad. The ad comes from Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads. You choose whether ads can be personalised, and whether apps may track you, and the game plays exactly the same either way.

If you buy Remove Ads, the ad is gone and the ad software from Google never starts. Apple handles the purchase, and RevenueCat records it under an anonymous ID.

We, DevPulse, do not run servers for TwinFin and do not receive personal data about you. Your scores and settings stay on your phone. No account, no analytics.

Who we are

TwinFin is made by DevPulse, the trading name of Devpulse Limited, based in the United Kingdom, at devpulse.co.uk. In this policy, we and us mean DevPulse. For the data that the banner ad and the purchase involve, Google and RevenueCat process it as described below and in their own policies.

What stays on your phone

The game keeps a small save file on your device only. It holds

  • your best score for each daily level, your all time best, how many tries you made each day and the medals you earned,
  • your settings for sound, music and vibration,
  • whether you bought Remove Ads, so the ad stays off when you are offline.

This file is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. The backup of your device, for example iCloud, may include it, under the terms of Apple.

The daily level is worked out on your phone from the date in UTC. The game needs no internet connection for that.

The banner ad, from Google AdMob

Unless you have bought Remove Ads, TwinFin shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen, provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google software in the app collects

  • device identifiers, which means the advertising identifier of Apple, called the IDFA, only if you allow tracking as described below, plus identifiers limited to the app or to us,
  • approximate location, estimated from your IP address and never from GPS,
  • advertising and usage data, such as which ads were shown, taps on them and app launches,
  • diagnostics, which means crash and performance data of the ad software.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. To see how Google uses it, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and the Google privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

TwinFin asks Google for ads suitable for a general audience only.

Your choices for consent and tracking

  • Consent in the UK, the EEA and Switzerland. Before any ad is requested, the consent form from Google, called the User Messaging Platform, asks whether Google and its partners may use your data for personalised ads and the other purposes listed there. You can change your answer at any time in TwinFin. Open Settings and tap Ad privacy choices.
  • App Tracking Transparency in all countries. iOS then asks whether TwinFin may track you across apps and websites of other companies. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and TwinFin asks Google for non personalised ads only. You can change this later in the Settings app of your iPhone, under Privacy and Security, then Tracking.
  • Neither question ever interrupts a run, and saying no does not change the game.
  • You can also reset your advertising identifier, or turn off personalised ads from Apple, in the Settings app under Privacy and Security, then Apple Advertising.

Remove Ads, an in app purchase through RevenueCat

Remove Ads is a one time purchase. Apple takes the payment, and we never see your card or Apple ID details. To check the purchase and restore it on your other devices, TwinFin uses RevenueCat, which receives the purchase receipt and keeps a purchase record, with the product, its date and the transaction details from the store, under an anonymous ID it creates for the app. We do not give RevenueCat your name, email or any account. The RevenueCat privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

After you buy Remove Ads, the consent form and the tracking prompt are no longer shown and the Google ad software is not started.

Game Center leaderboards

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, TwinFin can post your daily score to its leaderboards and show you how you rank. This is provided by Apple, not by us. Apple receives your score and your player profile, such as your nickname, under its own privacy policy, and the leaderboards show your nickname and score to other players. We do not receive your Game Center account details. The Apple privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy. You can turn Game Center off, or change who can see you, in the Settings app under Game Center. You can play TwinFin fully without it.

Sharing your score

The Share button opens the share sheet of your iPhone with a picture of your score card. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store on our servers or sell personal data, and there is no analytics or crash reporting software of our own in the game.
  • TwinFin does not ask for your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location. The iPhone may list some of these because the game engine includes them, but the game never uses them.

App Store

Apple handles downloads and updates and may collect data about that under its own policies. We see the anonymous, aggregated statistics that Apple shows every developer, for example downloads per country, which do not identify you.

Legal bases under UK GDPR and GDPR

  • For personalised ads and the advertising identifier, the basis is your consent, given through the consent form and the tracking prompt. You can withdraw it at any time as described above.
  • For non personalised ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention, the basis is our legitimate interest in funding a free game, and that of Google, as described in the Google policies.
  • For the Remove Ads purchase record, the basis is the performance of the contract of the purchase, so that it works on your devices and can be restored.

Children

TwinFin is rated for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children, it asks Google for ads suitable for a general audience only, and the purchase goes through the controls of Apple, for example Ask to Buy for family accounts.

How long data is kept

The save file stays on your phone until you delete the app. Google and RevenueCat keep the data described above for the periods set out in their policies.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of TwinFin changes what is collected, we will update this page and its date before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will change with it.

Contact

For questions about privacy in TwinFin, email support@devpulse.co.uk.
