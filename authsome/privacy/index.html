Privacy Policy

AuthSome collects nothing. Your Accounts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Last updated 19 November 2026

The Short Version

DevPulse runs no servers for AuthSome. There is nothing to sign up for, and no ads, tracking or code from other companies in the app. We never receive your Accounts, the keys behind them or your 2FA codes.

Who We Are

AuthSome is a free authenticator app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It makes the codes for two factor authentication, or 2FA, on your device, and is coming soon to the App Store. It is made by DevPulse, the trading name of Devpulse Limited, based in the United Kingdom, at devpulse.co.uk. In this policy, we and us mean DevPulse.

On Your Devices

Everything you add to AuthSome is kept on your device.

  • Secrets and recovery codes are kept in the Keychain on your device, which encrypts them and only releases them while the device is unlocked. A Secret is the key a service gives you once. The 2FA codes for that Account are made from it.
  • Issuers, Account names, Websites, and the names of your Profiles and Folders are kept in the app, in storage that your device encrypts. An Issuer is the service, such as Google. The Account name is what you sign in with there, and the Website is where you type its 2FA codes. Profiles and Folders are how you sort your Accounts.
  • 2FA codes are worked out on your device from the Secret and the time. They are never stored and never sent anywhere.
  • Your settings, such as the Theme and when the app locks, are kept on the device they were chosen on.

None of this is sent to us.

In Your iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud and leave iCloud turned on for AuthSome, your Profiles, Folders and Accounts are backed up to the private iCloud database that belongs to your own Apple Account, and appear on your other devices.

  • Everything you add is end to end encrypted before it leaves your device. That covers Secrets, Issuers, Account names, Websites, recovery codes, and the names of Profiles and Folders. Apple cannot read it and neither can we.
  • The only parts not encrypted this way are what iCloud needs to keep things in order, such as the position of an Account in its Folder and when it last changed.
  • This data is held by Apple under the terms of iCloud. We have no access to it.

You can turn iCloud off for AuthSome at any time in Settings on your device. AuthSome then works fully on that device alone.

What AuthSome Uses on Your Device

  • Camera. Used only while you scan a QR code to add an Account. The picture is read on your device and is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Photos and files. When you choose an image of a QR code or a backup file, AuthSome reads only the item you chose, on your device.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and your passcode. Your device checks these and tells AuthSome only whether the check passed. AuthSome never sees your face, your fingerprint or your passcode.
  • Clipboard. A copied 2FA code stays on the device you copied it on, and is cleared after a minute, unless you choose otherwise in Settings.
  • AutoFill. If you turn it on, your device asks AuthSome for a 2FA code when a sign in page needs one. AuthSome hands the 2FA code to your device after you confirm with Face ID or Touch ID. It keeps no record of the websites you sign in to.

Export Files

An export file is made on your device and saved where you choose. By default it is encrypted with a password that only you know. We cannot recover that password, so keep it somewhere safe. AuthSome also offers a plain export with no encryption, behind a clear warning. Anyone who has that file can make your 2FA codes, so treat it with care.

What AuthSome Does Not Do

  • No tracking, no ads and no analytics sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No code from other companies in the app.
  • No connections to the internet apart from your own iCloud. The logos of services are built in to the app, so it never looks them up on the web.
  • No sign up and no purchases.

Notes in the System Log

AuthSome notes how its features are used, for example that a 2FA code was copied, in the system log on your own device. The system log is a record that your device keeps for diagnosing problems. These notes stay on the device, are never sent, and never contain a 2FA code, a Secret or anything you typed.

App Store

Apple handles downloads and updates and may collect data about that under its own policies. We see the anonymous, combined statistics that Apple shows every developer, for example downloads per country. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in Settings on your device, Apple may also pass us anonymous crash reports. Neither identifies you.

If You Contact Us

If you email us for help, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to answer you, and keep it no longer than we need to. Please never send us a Secret, a QR code or a recovery code. We will never ask for one.

Your Rights

Data protection law in the UK and the EU gives you rights over personal data held about you, including to see it, correct it and have it deleted. Because AuthSome sends us nothing, the only personal data we can hold is an email you chose to send us. Write to the address below to use any of these rights. Your Accounts are in your own hands. You can export or delete them in the app at any time.

Children

AuthSome is a general utility and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, whatever their age.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of AuthSome changes what is collected, we will update this page and its date before that version is released, and the privacy details in the App Store will change with it.
