Do Authenticator Apps Transfer to a New iPhone?

It depends on the app. The app itself will install again on a new iPhone, but your 2FA codes only come with it if that app syncs or backs them up, and several apps need a setting turned on before you switch. Keep the old iPhone, unwiped, until every 2FA code works on the new one.

By Junaid Younus. Published 19 November 2026.

Why the Answer Depends on the App

An authenticator app makes each code for two factor authentication, or 2FA, from a secret key that the service gave you when you first scanned its QR code. This guide calls it the Secret. Moving to a new iPhone only works if the Secrets move too, and each app keeps and moves them in its own way.

So there is no single rule for Quick Start or for restoring from an iCloud backup. Most makers do not say what a phone to phone transfer carries, and this guide does not guess. Go by what the maker of your app says, then check on the new iPhone.

Google Authenticator

Google says that if you are signed in to a Google Account in the app, your 2FA codes are saved to that account. On the new iPhone, install the app, tap Get Started and sign in to the same Google Account. If the 2FA codes were synced, they appear.

If you use it without an account, the 2FA codes are only on the old phone and you move them by QR code. Make the export QR codes on the old phone, as set out in how to export Google Authenticator to another app. On the new iPhone, open the menu, choose Transfer accounts, then Import accounts, and scan each one.

Microsoft Authenticator

Microsoft says the old iPhone needs iCloud Drive, iCloud Keychain and iCloud Backup turned on, and Authenticator switched on in your Saved to iCloud list. It also says to update the app to version 6.8.33 or later and to open it at least once before you switch.

Not everything returns. Microsoft says the 30 second 2FA codes for personal Microsoft accounts and for other services are restored. A personal account that uses passwordless sign in comes back as a name only, and you must sign in again. So does a work or school account, which you must also register again. A backup made on an iPhone cannot be restored on Android.

Apple Passwords

Apple says the passwords, passkeys and verification codes in the Passwords app are available on every device where you sign in to the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud Keychain in iCloud settings. If your 2FA codes live in Passwords, check that iCloud Keychain is on before you switch.

2FAS

2FAS says that if iCloud Backup is on for the iPhone, 2FAS is stored in that backup and restored with it. It also recommends its own iCloud sync. Moving between Android and iPhone uses an export file instead, covered in moving 2FA codes from Android to iPhone.

Ente Auth

Ente says that 2FA codes kept under Use without backups are never synced to Ente and cannot be restored from its servers, and that device transfers and system backups are not supported ways to recover them. If that is how you use it, make an encrypted export from the Data settings before you switch. If you use an Ente account, Ente says your 2FA codes sync to every device where you sign in to it.

Authy

Authy says its backups are encrypted with a password that only you know, and that it cannot recover that password. So Authy needs backups turned on, and you need to know that password. That page does not give the steps for a new phone, so follow the prompts in the app.

A Safe Order for Switching iPhones

Do not erase, trade in or give away the old iPhone until step 6. If the 2FA codes did not come across and the old phone is wiped, you have to recover each service one by one.

  1. On the old iPhone, turn on what your app needs from the table, then open the app once.
  2. Save the recovery codes for the services you could least afford to lose, such as your email. Most services offer them in their security settings.
  3. Set up the new iPhone in whichever way you prefer. Leave the old one switched on and untouched.
  4. Open the authenticator app on the new iPhone and sign in if it asks.
  5. Check that every Account is listed. Then hold the two phones side by side. Where both show the same 2FA code at the same moment, that Secret came across. Sign in to one service with a 2FA code from the new iPhone to be sure.
  6. Only when every Account passes, erase the old iPhone.

To keep your 2FA codes on both phones for good, see using the same authenticator app on two devices.

If 2FA Codes Are Missing on the New iPhone

Stop and keep the old iPhone as it is. Then work through these.

  • Google Authenticator. Google says to tap the profile picture and check that you are still signed in, and that the 2FA codes were not saved under a different Google Account.
  • Microsoft Authenticator. Microsoft says that if the backup does not appear, delete the app from the new iPhone and install it again. Work or school accounts always need registering again.
  • Any app, with the old iPhone still working. Go back to the old phone, turn on the setting you missed, or use the export in the app.
  • The old iPhone is gone. For 2FA codes in Google Authenticator that were not synced, Google says to visit each site where you set it up, remove the old 2FA codes and link the new device. Expect the same with any app that kept no backup. You sign in with a recovery code, or go through the account recovery of that service, then set up 2FA again.

Common Questions

Does Quick Start Move 2FA Codes to a New iPhone?

It depends on the app, and most makers do not say. Treat Quick Start as unproven for your app until you have compared the 2FA codes on both phones.

Can I Transfer My Authenticator App Without the Old Phone?

Only if the app synced or backed up your 2FA codes before the old phone was lost. If it did not, use your recovery codes or the account recovery of each service, then set up 2FA again on the new iPhone.

Does Microsoft Authenticator Transfer From Android to a New iPhone?

No. Microsoft says a backup can only be restored on the same type of device, so each account has to be added again on the iPhone.

Should I Delete the Authenticator App From My Old iPhone?

Not yet. Deleting the app can delete the only copy of your 2FA codes, so wait until every Account has worked on the new iPhone.
