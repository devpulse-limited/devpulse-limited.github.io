Authy Desktop Is Gone. What to Use on a Mac Instead

Authy Desktop for Mac reached its end of life on 19 March 2024. To get 2FA codes on a Mac now, use Apple Passwords, which is built in, or an app with a Mac version such as Ente Auth or Proton Authenticator. Authy has no export, so you move by setting up two factor authentication, or 2FA, again at each service while Authy still works on your phone.

By Junaid Younus at DevPulse. Published 19 November 2026. Facts last checked 19 November 2026.

What Happened to Authy Desktop?

Twilio, the company that makes Authy, announced the change in its changelog on 19 February 2024. The Authy Desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux would reach their end of life on 19 March 2024. The same notice says the date had been August 2024 and was brought forward.

The Authy system requirements page now lists the apps for Linux, Mac and Windows computers as deprecated, and says Twilio does not update them any more. The platforms it lists as officially supported are Android, iPhone and iPad.

If you find an Authy Desktop installer on a download site, leave it alone. The same Twilio page names the Apple App Store and the Google Play Store as the places to get Authy, and a desktop installer from anywhere else is software its maker has stopped updating.

Which Mac App Should Replace Authy?

Authy users were used to the same 2FA codes on a computer and a phone, so look for an app that has a real Mac version and keeps your devices in step. This is what each maker says on its own pages, read on 19 November 2026.

Apple Passwords is part of macOS Sequoia 15 and later, so it needs nothing installed. Ente describes Ente Auth as free and open source, with end to end encrypted sync. Proton says Proton Authenticator is free on every platform, with end to end encrypted sync if you sign in with a Proton Account.

None of these gives you a shortcut out of Authy. Proton names Authy on its App Store page as an app you can move from, but its import guide lists no Authy option. That guide says that where an app does not allow exporting, you create new 2FA codes in Proton Authenticator. Twilio says Authy does not allow exporting, so expect to set up each service again.

Can You Still Use Authy on a Mac?

Not in any way Twilio supports. Its system requirements page lists the Mac only under deprecated items, and it says that a device missing from the supported list is not officially supported and may have usability problems.

The changelog does not say whether a copy of Authy Desktop that is already installed still opens or shows 2FA codes. If yours does, do not count on it lasting, and use the time to move.

If the App Store on your Mac offers you Authy, the same warning applies. The Mac is not on the supported list, so Twilio does not promise that Authy will work there or keep working.

What does still work is Authy on your phone. You can keep it there and type each 2FA code into your Mac by hand. If that is enough for you, there is nothing to move. If you want your 2FA codes on the Mac itself, you need another app.

Does Authy Have an Export?

No. A Twilio help page, checked on 19 November 2026, says the Authy app does not allow importing or exporting tokens, its word for the services you added, and gives security as the reason.

The route Twilio gives instead is to sign in to each service, turn 2FA off, turn it on again, and scan the new QR code with the app you are moving to.

That page gives no other route. Be wary of any script or tool that claims to pull the secret keys out of Authy. A secret key is what a service gave the app to make your 2FA codes from, so running unknown software against all of them is a risk in itself.

So the real cost of leaving Authy is time. There is no bulk move. Each service has to be visited once, and a service run by your employer may need your IT team to reset 2FA for you.

How to Move From Authy to a Mac App, One Service at a Time

Caution

Do not delete Authy from your phone, remove a service from inside Authy, or delete your Authy account until every service works with the new app. Twilio warns that if you delete your Authy account while a service still has its 2FA tied to Authy, you may be unable to sign in to that service. Before you change a service, save its recovery codes, which most services offer in their security settings.

  1. Install the new app on your Mac and open it once, so you know it runs.
  2. Choose one service. Sign in to it on your Mac, using the 2FA code from Authy on your phone.
  3. At that service, open the security settings and find 2FA, which some services call two step verification. Some let you change the authenticator app directly. Otherwise turn 2FA off, then turn it on again straight away.
  4. The service shows a QR code, and usually the secret key in letters and numbers as well. Add it to the new app. A Mac app cannot point a camera at its own screen, so use a screenshot of the QR code if your app reads images, or copy the secret key.
  5. Type the 2FA code from the new app into the service to finish.
  6. If the service gives you new recovery codes, save them somewhere safe. The old ones may no longer work.
  7. Sign out and sign in again with a 2FA code from the new app. When that works, this service is done.
  8. Go on to the next service. Start with your email, since other services send their reset links there.

Leave 2FA off only for as long as the change takes. Finish each service before you start another.

When every service works from the new app, you can remove Authy. Twilio says deleting an Authy account runs over a 30 day period with confirmation emails, and cannot be reversed once it completes.

Common Questions

Is Authy Desktop Still Available to Download?

Not from Twilio. Its help pages list only the iPhone, iPad and Android apps as supported, and name the App Store and Google Play as the places to get them. Copies on other download sites are the ended desktop app.

Can You Import Authy Into Another Authenticator App?

No. Twilio says Authy does not allow exporting tokens, its word for the services you added, so another app has nothing to read. Each service has to be set up again with a new QR code.

What Happens If You Delete Authy Before Moving Your Accounts?

You may be locked out of every service that still expects an Authy 2FA code. Your way back in is the recovery codes each service gave you, or its own support team.

What Is a Good Authy Alternative on iPhone as Well as Mac?

Pick one app that runs on both and syncs between them, so you set up each service once. Apple Passwords, Ente Auth and Proton Authenticator all have iPhone and Mac apps.
