Getting started

How do I add an Account?

Turn on two step verification at the service you want to protect. It shows a QR code. In AuthSome, tap +, then Scan QR Code, and point your camera at it. Check the 2FA code AuthSome shows against the service, then add it.

The QR code is on the same device

Take a screenshot of it. Tap +, then Scan QR Code, and choose the screenshot from Photos. On a Mac, choose QR Code Image, or drag the image on to the window. If the service shows a setup link or a Secret as text, copy it and use Paste Link or Enter Manually.

How do I move over from another app?

Tap +, then Import Accounts. AuthSome reads the export QR codes of Google Authenticator, backup files from 2FAS and Aegis, a text file of setup links, and its own backups. You choose the Profile, and you see everything that was found before anything is added.

For Google Authenticator, open its menu, choose Transfer accounts, then Export accounts, and scan each code it shows. A 2FAS backup has to be exported without a password.

Profiles and Folders

How do I make a Profile?

Tap the name of the Profile at the top, then New Profile. To change between Profiles, swipe sideways anywhere on the screen, or pick one from the same menu.

How do I move an Account?

Touch and hold the Account, choose Move to, then pick a Profile or a Folder. You can also drag it. Every move can be undone from the message at the bottom of the screen.

Why can I not find an Account?

Search looks in the Profile you are in. If the Account is in another Profile, the results say so and offer to take you there. If you deleted it in the last 30 days, look in Recently Deleted, at the bottom of the Profile menu.

AutoFill

How do I turn on AutoFill?

  1. In AuthSome, open Settings, then AutoFill, then tap Open Settings.
  2. Turn on AuthSome.
  3. Choose AuthSome for Set Up Codes In.

From then on, when a sign in page asks for your 2FA code, AuthSome offers it above the keyboard.

AutoFill does not offer my 2FA code

AutoFill matches by the Website saved with the Account. Open the Account details and check that Website is the address you sign in at, for example zentrix.test.

2FA codes

A service says my 2FA code is wrong

2FA codes depend on the time. In the Settings of your device, under General, then Date and Time, make sure Set Automatically is on. If the 2FA code still fails, the service may have replaced the Secret. Turn two step verification off and on again there, and add the Account again.

Why did AuthSome copy the next 2FA code?

With fewer than five seconds left, AuthSome copies the next 2FA code so it does not expire while you paste it. Services accept it a few seconds early.

Can I change the Secret of an Account?

No. The Secret comes from the service. To change it, add the Account again from a new QR code and delete the old one.

Backup and new devices

How do I get my Accounts on a new device?

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, install AuthSome and open it. Your Profiles, Folders and Accounts arrive by themselves. Settings, then Backup, shows when they were last backed up.

AuthSome says Not backed up

Your Accounts are safe on this device, but not yet in iCloud. Open Settings, then Backup, to see why. Most often iCloud is signed out, turned off for AuthSome, or full.

How do I make a backup file?

Open Settings, then Export Accounts, and choose a password. Keep the password somewhere safe. Nobody, including us, can open the file without it.

I lost my device and have no backup

We hold no copy of your Accounts, so we cannot restore them. Use the recovery codes each service gave you when you set it up, or contact the service to prove who you are.

Lock and privacy

How does the lock work?

AuthSome asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when you open it. In Settings, under Security, you can choose how long you can be away before it locks again.

An Account has the wrong logo or colour

The Brand is matched from the Issuer and the Website. Correct either one in the Account details and the Brand follows. An Issuer that AuthSome does not know gets its initials, in a colour you can choose.

What do you collect?

Nothing. The privacy policy has the details.

Something is not right

  • Close AuthSome and open it again.
