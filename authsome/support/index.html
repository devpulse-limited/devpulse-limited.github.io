About AuthSome

What Does AuthSome Do?

Many websites and apps ask for a short code after your password when you sign in. This is called two factor authentication, or 2FA. AuthSome makes those 2FA codes on your device, and keeps your personal and work ones apart.

Is AuthSome Free?

Yes. There are no ads, no purchases and nothing to sign up for.

Which Devices Does AuthSome Work On?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later, an iPad with iPadOS 26 or later, and a Mac with macOS 26 or later. It comes in 44 languages. There is no Android or Apple Watch version.

When Can I Get AuthSome?

AuthSome is not on the App Store yet. It is coming soon.

Getting Started

How Do I Add an Account?

An Account is one service you have added, such as Google. You add each one once.

  1. At the service, turn on 2FA. Some services call it two step verification.
  2. The service shows a QR code. In AuthSome, tap +, then Scan QR Code, and point your camera at it.
  3. The service asks for a 2FA code to finish. Type in the one AuthSome shows, then add the Account.

What If the QR Code Is on the Same Device?

Take a screenshot of it. Tap +, then Scan QR Code, and choose the screenshot from Photos. On a Mac, choose QR Code Image, or drag the image on to the window.

What If There Is No QR Code?

If the service shows a line of letters and numbers, that is the Secret, the key your 2FA codes are made from. Tap +, then Enter Manually, and type or paste it in. If the service gives a setup link, copy it, then tap +, then Paste Link.

How Do I Move Over From Another App?

Tap +, then Import Accounts. AuthSome reads the export QR codes of Google Authenticator, backup files from 2FAS and Aegis, a text file of setup links, and its own backups. You choose the Profile, and you see everything that was found before anything is added.

For Google Authenticator, open its menu, choose Transfer accounts, then Export accounts, and scan each code it shows. A 2FAS backup has to be exported without a password.

Why Does an Account Have the Wrong Logo or Colour?

The logo and colour of an Account are its Brand. AuthSome picks the Brand from the name of the service, which it calls the Issuer, and from its Website. Correct either one in the Account details and the Brand follows. An Issuer that AuthSome does not know gets its initials, in a colour you can choose.

Profiles and Folders

How Do I Make a Profile?

A Profile is a separate set of Accounts, such as Personal or Work, and you see one Profile at a time. Tap the name of the Profile at the top, then New Profile. To change between Profiles, swipe sideways anywhere on the screen, or pick one from the same menu.

How Do I Move an Account?

Touch and hold the Account, choose Move to, then pick a Profile or a Folder. A Folder is a named section inside a Profile. You can also drag the Account. Every move can be undone from the message at the bottom of the screen.

Why Can I Not Find an Account?

Search looks in the Profile you are in. If the Account is in another Profile, the results say so and offer to take you there. If you deleted it in the last 30 days, look in Recently Deleted, at the bottom of the Profile menu.

AutoFill

How Do I Turn On AutoFill?

  1. In AuthSome, open Settings, then AutoFill, then tap Open Settings. This opens Settings on your device.
  2. Turn on AuthSome.
  3. Under Set Up Codes In, choose AuthSome.

From then on, when a sign in page asks for your 2FA code, AuthSome offers it above the keyboard.

Why Does AutoFill Not Offer My 2FA Code?

AutoFill matches by the Website saved with the Account. Open the Account details and check that Website is the address you sign in at, for example example.com.

2FA Codes

How Do I Use a 2FA Code?

When a service asks for your 2FA code, open AuthSome and find that Account. Copy its 2FA code and paste it in to the sign in page, or read it and type it in.

AuthSome shows your Accounts as a Grid or a List. An iPhone starts with the Grid, and an iPad or a Mac with the List. In the Grid, tap the Account and its 2FA code shows large at the top. Tap that 2FA code, or Copy, to copy it. In the List, tap the Account to copy its 2FA code. On a Mac, click the 2FA code itself in the List, as a click on the rest of the row only selects it.

A 2FA code lasts a short time, usually 30 seconds, and the ring round the icon shows how much is left. With AutoFill turned on, you can skip all of this. On a Mac, AuthSome can also sit in the menu bar, where a click on an Account copies its 2FA code.

Why Does a Service Say My 2FA Code Is Wrong?

2FA codes depend on the time. In Settings on your device, under General, then Date and Time, make sure Set Automatically is on. If the 2FA code still fails, the service may have replaced the Secret. Turn 2FA off and on again there, and add the Account again.

Why Did AuthSome Copy the Next 2FA Code?

With fewer than five seconds left, AuthSome copies the next 2FA code so it does not expire while you paste it. Services accept it a few seconds early.

Can I Change the Secret of an Account?

No. The Secret comes from the service. To change it, add the Account again from a new QR code and delete the old one.

Backup and New Devices

How Do I Get My Accounts on a New Device?

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, install AuthSome and open it. Your Profiles, Folders and Accounts arrive by themselves. Settings, then Backup, shows when they were last backed up.

Why Does AuthSome Say Not Backed Up?

Your Accounts are safe on this device, but not yet in iCloud. Open Settings, then Backup, to see why. Most often iCloud is signed out, turned off for AuthSome, or full.

How Do I Make a Backup File?

Open Settings, then Export Accounts, and choose a password. Keep the password somewhere safe. Nobody, including us, can open the file without it.

Where Can I Keep Recovery Codes?

Recovery codes are the backup codes a service gives you in case you cannot use your 2FA codes. You can keep them with the Account, in its details, under Recovery codes. AuthSome asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode each time you view or change them.

What If I Lose My Device and Have No Backup?

We hold no copy of your Accounts, so we cannot restore them. Use the recovery codes each service gave you when you set it up, or contact the service to prove who you are.

Lock and Privacy

How Does the Lock Work?

AuthSome asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when you open it. On a Mac, your password stands in for the passcode. In Settings, under Security, you can choose how long you can be away before it locks again.

What Do You Collect?

Nothing. The privacy policy has the details.
