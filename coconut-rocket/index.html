How it plays

Hold. Steer. Climb!

An endless arcade climber that fits under one thumb. Weave up a level packed with traps, coins and close calls, and never slow down.

  1. 01

    Hold to Fly

    Press anywhere and the thrusters roar. Let go and you start to drop, straight towards the poison.

  2. 02

    Slide to Steer

    Slide your thumb left and right to weave past spiked logs, swinging blades, bats, spiders and jets of fire.

  3. 03

    Outrun the Poison

    Slow down and the poison swallows you. Chain coins and skim past danger to push your score higher.

Three zones

From Jungle to Magma.

Rise from the jungle, through the caves and into the magma. Every zone has its own look and its own soundtrack.

  • Zone 1

    The Jungle

    Vines, roots and fireflies. A gentle start, so enjoy it while it lasts.

  • Zone 2

    The Caves

    Crystals, dripping rock and a lot less room to breathe.

  • Zone 3

    The Magma

    Molten rock and rising heat. Only the bravest pilots get this far.

A new level every day

Same Level. Everyone.

Every day brings a fresh daily level, and it is the same one for every player. Chase your best score, and come back each day to keep your streak alive for bigger coin rewards.

Want to warm up first? Random runs give you a fresh layout every time.

Good to know

Quick Questions.

Is Coconut Rocket free?

Yes, it is free to download and play. Coins are earned by playing, and every character and rocket is bought with coins.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later. An Android version is on the way.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account and nothing to sign up for. Your progress stays on your phone, and you can play offline.

What happens to my data?

Your coins, crew, scores and settings stay on your phone. The privacy policy has all the details.
