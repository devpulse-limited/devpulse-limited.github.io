01 · Overview

What DriveByTheory is

Nearly 2.8 million car theory tests were conducted in Great Britain in 2024 to 2025, and fewer than half resulted in a pass (44.9%, DVSA statistical data set, table DRT111C). Each failed attempt costs the learner £23 and, with practical test waiting lists at record lengths, months of delay. DriveByTheory exists to get learners through first time.

The app combines three things no current product offers together: complete official revision content licensed from DVSA, a hazard perception trainer that coaches timing, replaying every clip against its scoring window and explaining exactly why a response was too early, too late, or just right, and a personal countdown plan built backwards from the learner's booked test date, with daily sessions, spaced-repetition review, and an honest readiness meter.

The product is fully offline after download: no account, no advertising, and a single one-time price. It is aimed at learners aged 17 to 24 and designed, visually and tonally, to feel premium, calm, and encouraging, with a friendly traffic cone brand character ("Don Coneleone") providing personality in celebrations and study reminders.

02 · Licensed content

How DVSA material is used

DriveByTheory requests a standard licence for the car revision question bank and hazard perception clips. Licensed material is used respectfully, completely, and only inside the app:

  • Revision questions are presented verbatim, each accompanied by its official knowledge-and-understanding text, displayed with every question as the licence requires. Our original study aids (plain-English explanations, notes on why each incorrect option is wrong, and memory hooks) appear alongside the official text, clearly separated from it, and never replacing it.
  • Hazard perception clips (MPEG-4, with official scoring-window timeframes) power both full mock tests scored to the real scheme (5-4-3-2-1 within the window) and our training mode, which replays the learner's taps on a timeline against the scoring window with coaching feedback. The clips themselves are never altered; our overlay graphics render on top during replay only.
  • The Highway Code and traffic sign images are included under the Open Government Licence with attribution.
  • Content protection: all licensed material ships inside the app package in a non-user-accessible format and is viewable only through the app. The app provides no facility to export, copy, share, or print licensed content, and there is no API and no web version.
  • Honesty to learners: the app describes questions as official DVSA revision questions and clips as official practice clips. It never claims to contain the live test bank, and never implies DVSA endorsement.

04 · Brand & compliance

A distinct, non-official identity

App icon and brand character. DriveByTheory's identity is a friendly cartoon traffic cone in a fedora, known as "Don Coneleone", rendered in the app's own racing-green and cone-orange palette. The character brings warmth and personality to studying, appearing in celebrations, reminders, and coaching moments, for a young audience.

05 · Business plan

Model, forecast, and royalty handling

Model: free download with a substantial free preview; a single £5.99 in-app purchase unlocks all content permanently. No subscription, no advertising, no in-app promotion of third parties. This pricing follows the category's established norm and keeps the product affordable for young learners.
