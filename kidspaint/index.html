Little Questions, Answered.

Is Kids Paint free?+

Kids Paint is free to download and use, with a collection of free templates and all painting tools ready to enjoy out of the box. More templates are added regularly and can be unlocked with a subscription. Prices and renewal details will be shown in the app before you buy.

Can we use it on a phone or tablet?+

Yes. Kids Paint is designed for iPhone, iPad, Android phones, and Android tablets, with a roomy canvas and simple controls.

Where do the pictures go?+

Artwork saves on the device, ready to open and keep painting. A grown up can share or export a picture. Export the favourites you want to keep.

Does the app collect analytics?+

Any usage analytics are anonymous and help improve the app. They do not include drawings, entered text, or credentials. Purchases are handled separately by the billing provider and the store on your device.
