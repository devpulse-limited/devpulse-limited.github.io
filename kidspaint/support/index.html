Say Hello

Help Us Help You

Tell us what happened, your device model, and your app version, if you know it. A short description of the steps is usually enough.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, parent PINs, or personal information about a child.

A Purchase Question?

Try Restore Purchases on the subscription screen using the same store account. You can manage or cancel an active subscription through the store on your device.
